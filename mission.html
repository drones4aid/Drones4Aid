View Mission Past Missions

Mission

DRONES4AID is focused on protecting civilians in war-affected and war-prone areas by supplying food, essentials, and emergency relief where help is hardest to reach.

What We Do

We deploy drones to move small, urgent relief items and monitor operations through our own Drones4Aid app, which will be released later.

Our Commitment

Our mission is reviewed regularly so it stays safe, lawful, and effective. We aim to reduce suffering and help save 25% of people in danger in war zones by 2030.
