About DRONES4AID

We are a non-profit, non-governmental humanitarian organisation committed to peace, aid, and support for people affected by crisis in war-affected and war-prone regions.

Who We Are

DRONES4AID works to support vulnerable communities through humanitarian assistance, responsible drone-based support, and community-focused relief efforts. Our work is driven by compassion, safety, and dignity.

Our Overall Goal

Our overall goal is to reduce suffering, deliver essential aid efficiently, and support peace-building efforts in communities affected by conflict, displacement, and emergency situations.

Our Base

Our base of operations is in Gurgaon, India, where our team coordinates planning, communication, logistics, and outreach.

Our Team

Our team includes humanitarian volunteers, logistics coordinators, research members, community outreach workers, and drone support staff working together for one purpose: to bring relief where it is needed most.

Leadership Team

Guides the organisation’s mission, partnerships, and long-term humanitarian goals.

Operations Team

Handles aid coordination, packaging, planning, and safe delivery support.

Volunteer Team

Supports outreach, fundraising, communication, and on-ground humanitarian work.

Our Vast Locations

We focus on humanitarian support across many crisis-affected regions around the world. Our reach is built around partnerships, need, and safe operational conditions.
