Drone Models

A catalog-style view of the models we use and study for humanitarian and operational work.

DJI Mini 4 Pro

The DJI Mini 4 Pro is DJI's flagship sub-249g lightweight drone, typically priced around ₹81,000 for the aircraft only and upwards of ₹99,999 for combo kits with the RC 2 screen controller. It features 4K/60fps HDR video, omnidirectional obstacle sensing, and O4 video transmission up to 20 km.

DJI Mini Pro 5

The DJI Mini 5 Pro is an ultra-light, foldable camera drone featuring a large 1-inch 50MP CMOS sensor, nightscape omnidirectional obstacle sensing with forward LiDAR, 225° flexible gimbal rotation, and upgraded ActiveTrack 360°. It typically sells for around ₹1,14,999 to ₹1,41,990 depending on the bundle.

DJI Neo

The DJI Neo is an ultra-lightweight, palm-sized mini drone designed specifically for vloggers, beginners, and casual content creators. It serves as a seamless alternative to a selfie stick or tripod, allowing users to capture effortless aerial footage without needing complex piloting skills.

DJI Mavic 4 Pro

The DJI Mavic 4 Pro is a flagship triple-camera drone featuring a 100MP 4/3 CMOS Hasselblad main camera, a 360° rotating Infinity Gimbal supporting native vertical video, 0.1-Lux Nightscape omnidirectional obstacle sensing with LiDAR, 51-minute max flight time, and a 30 km O4+ transmission range.

IZI Bephos

The IZI Brephos is a lineup of premium consumer and professional camera drones designed by the Indian consumer tech brand IZI. The series is designed around advanced low-light video capabilities, long transmission ranges, and stabilization features.

Drone Manufacturing

In-House Factory Production

We manufacture all of our drones inside our own factory, which gives us control over quality, testing, and assembly. Every stage is checked carefully so the final product is reliable, safe, and ready for real operations.

Our manufacturing process focuses on precision, durability, and repeatable performance for humanitarian use.

We follow component-level inspection, so motors, batteries, sensors, and flight systems are tested before assembly.

We also use standardized quality checks to make sure each drone performs consistently across different environments.

Final build verification helps confirm flight stability, payload safety, and navigation readiness before deployment.

Drone Monitoring

Drones4Aid App Control

We monitor our drones through our very own Drones4Aid app. Each drone is inspected carefully, and all drones run on automatic systems that support stable navigation, tracking, and mission oversight.

The monitoring system helps us keep flights organized, observe performance, and respond quickly if a route needs to be adjusted.

Telemetry data helps track battery health, altitude, speed, and signal strength in real time.

Automated alerts support faster intervention when a drone needs maintenance, recalibration, or route correction.

Mission logs help us review flight behavior, improve reliability, and plan future deployments more accurately.

Drone Deployment

Safe Deployment Network

We deploy our drones from safe locations and use control towers to navigate them through each mission. Since our drones can only fly for a maximum of 30 kms, we place charging stations every 30 kms to keep operations continuous and controlled.

Our deployment areas are planned around humanitarian access routes, secure corridors, and locations where speed and safety matter most.

We are also preserving ecological diversity by using a harmless process that reduces unnecessary disturbance to the environment.

We are trying to innovate more solar-powered drones, and we shall start deployment of solar-powered drones from 2028.

Deployment planning also considers weather, terrain, and civilian safety so the route remains reliable and low-risk.
