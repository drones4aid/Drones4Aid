Operations

Ongoing Operations Across War-Affected and War-Prone Areas

We operate in several war-prone and war-affected areas, focusing on civilian humanitarian support, access assessments, and urgent relief delivery based on stress scale and need.

Operations

We operate in several war-prone and war-affected areas, including Russia, Ukraine, Burkina Faso, Niger, Sudan, South Sudan, Mali, Pakistan, Afghanistan, the Baloch region, Iran, Lebanon, Palestine, and Israel.

Urgency Scale

Our operations are divided into three classes based on a war-affected and war-prone stress scale.

1–3 Mild
4–6 Medium
7–10 Severe
Low Moderate Critical

How We Classify

  • Severe urgency (7–10): critical humanitarian situations requiring the highest level of response.
  • Medium urgency (4–6): serious humanitarian pressure where additional support may be required.
  • Mild urgency (1–3): lower-level support needs where essential services are more accessible.
  • Assessment factors: civilian need, infrastructure damage, displacement, access to essentials, and operational safety.

Ongoing Operations

According to the stress scale, we take more drastic measures where needed, but we treat every affected area equally and with the same humanitarian purpose.

Severe Urgency

1. Israel and Palestine

Stress level: 8.5

The Situation

The Israel–Palestine conflict has created severe humanitarian challenges, with civilians facing shortages of food, clean water, medical supplies and basic necessities. Damage to infrastructure and disruption to transport routes have also made it difficult for aid to reach some communities quickly and consistently.


Our Operation

In our Israel–Palestine operation, DRONES4AID focused on providing direct humanitarian assistance to affected civilians. In Khan Younis, our teams delivered 250 emergency food packages to displaced families, while in Deir al-Balah we distributed 180 hygiene and essential-supply packages. In Rafah, a further 120 family food-and-water packages were provided to civilians requiring urgent assistance. Our teams also carried out humanitarian access assessments around Gaza City to identify damaged routes and areas where conventional aid delivery was difficult. Basic first-aid and emergency-relief packages were subsequently provided through designated civilian distribution points in areas where access was authorised and considered safe. Throughout the operation, DRONES4AID focused exclusively on civilian humanitarian assistance, prioritising food, water, hygiene supplies and basic medical essentials while maintaining safety, privacy and accountability.

Severe Urgency

2. Russia–Ukraine war tensions

Stress level: 8.2

The Situation

The Russia–Ukraine war has caused widespread displacement, damage to homes and infrastructure, and disruption to access to food, water and medical necessities. Communities close to heavily affected areas can face additional difficulties when roads, bridges and local supply networks are damaged or disrupted.


Our Operation

In our Russia–Ukraine humanitarian operation, DRONES4AID focused on supporting civilians affected by displacement and infrastructure damage. In Kharkiv, our teams delivered 300 emergency food packages containing basic non-perishable supplies to displaced families. In Kherson, we distributed 200 water and hygiene packages to households experiencing disruption to essential services. In Mykolaiv, DRONES4AID provided 150 emergency-support packages containing food, hygiene products and basic first-aid materials. Our teams also conducted humanitarian access assessments around damaged infrastructure to identify areas where conventional relief delivery was difficult. The operation prioritised civilian needs and used drone technology only for authorised humanitarian purposes, with no weapons, military targeting or combat activity involved.

Severe Urgency

3. The Sahel region conflict

Stress level: 7.5

The Situation

Conflict and insecurity across the Sahel region, particularly in areas of Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger, have displaced communities and disrupted access to food, clean water, healthcare and other basic necessities. Damaged infrastructure and insecurity can make conventional humanitarian deliveries difficult, particularly for communities located far from major distribution centres.


Our Operation

In our Sahel humanitarian operation, DRONES4AID focused on assisting civilians affected by displacement and shortages of essential supplies. In Mopti, Mali, our teams delivered 220 emergency food packages containing basic non-perishable supplies to displaced families. In Tillabéri, Niger, we distributed 160 water and hygiene packages to communities experiencing difficulties accessing essential services. In Djibo, Burkina Faso, DRONES4AID provided 140 emergency-relief packages containing food, hygiene products and basic medical supplies. Our teams also conducted humanitarian access assessments to identify communities where damaged infrastructure or difficult terrain made conventional delivery challenging. The operation remained focused exclusively on civilian assistance, with drone technology considered only for authorised humanitarian purposes and with safety, impartiality and protection of civilians kept at the centre of the programme.

Medium Urgency

4. The Sudanese Civil War

Stress scale: 6.3

The Situation

The Sudanese civil war has caused massive displacement and severe disruption to food supplies, healthcare, clean water and essential infrastructure. Communities in areas affected by fighting and displacement have faced particularly difficult access to humanitarian assistance.


Our Operation

In our Sudan humanitarian operation, DRONES4AID focused on supporting civilians displaced by the conflict and helping communities access essential supplies. In Khartoum, our teams delivered 200 emergency food packages containing basic non-perishable supplies to displaced families. In Omdurman, we distributed 150 water and hygiene packages to households experiencing disruption to essential services. In El Fasher, Darfur, DRONES4AID provided 120 emergency-relief packages containing food, hygiene materials and basic first-aid supplies. Our teams also carried out humanitarian access assessments to identify areas where damaged roads and infrastructure made conventional delivery difficult. Throughout the operation, our focus remained on civilian assistance, with drone technology used only for authorised humanitarian purposes and with safety, impartiality and protection of civilians prioritised.

Mild Urgency

5. Baloch Region Disputes

Stress scale: 3.2

The Situation

The Balochistan region of Pakistan has faced humanitarian pressures linked to insecurity, displacement, poverty, and natural disasters. In previous emergencies, heavy snowfall, rain and flooding have damaged homes, roads and water infrastructure, while communities have experienced shortages of food and clean drinking water. A UN-backed assessment, for example, identified food, WASH, shelter and healthcare as major needs in affected Balochistan districts.


Our Operation

In our Balochistan humanitarian operation, DRONES4AID focused on supporting civilians affected by disrupted access to essential supplies. In Quetta, our teams delivered 200 emergency food packages containing basic non-perishable supplies, while in Ziarat we distributed 150 water and hygiene packages to families affected by disruption to local services. In Pishin, DRONES4AID provided 120 emergency-relief packages containing food, hygiene materials and basic first-aid supplies. Our teams also carried out humanitarian access assessments around damaged roads and infrastructure to identify communities where conventional relief delivery was difficult. The operation remained focused on civilian humanitarian assistance, with safety, lawful access, impartiality and protection of affected communities treated as priorities.

DRONES4AID is committed to responding based on urgency, but every affected community is treated with equal dignity, respect and humanitarian purpose.
