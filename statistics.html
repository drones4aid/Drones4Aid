Statistics

These statistics are about our company and the way our humanitarian work grows across regions, goals, operations, and volunteer support.

They show where we work, what we are building, and how far our aid systems have progressed over time.

Use the sections below to explore the timeline, goals, maps, performance, and live counters.

Timeline

Click any dot to expand the event and see a short explanation of what happened in that period.

2016
Foundation phase

We began building the concept of a humanitarian drone network and mapped the first mission models.

The focus was on research, safety, and defining how technology could support civilians.

We also developed our early planning structure and tested operational ideas for future deployment.

2018
Regional planning

We expanded our humanitarian planning into conflict-affected regions and refined logistics workflows.

Teams began preparing for work in highly stressed environments with limited ground access.

Our protocols were improved to keep aid delivery safe, fast, and transparent.

2020
Sahel operations

We supported planning for the Sahel region, where access and infrastructure challenges were especially severe.

This helped us improve route planning, terrain analysis, and field coordination.

It also strengthened our role in high-need humanitarian environments.

2022
Ukraine and Russia border focus

We expanded attention to Ukraine, Russia-adjacent border conditions, and disrupted infrastructure zones.

The work centered on rapid assessment, urgent aid coordination, and safer movement of essential resources.

We also refined our response systems for unpredictable emergency conditions.

2023
Palestine and Israel emergency support

We strengthened our humanitarian planning for Palestine and Israel during periods of severe stress and disruption.

Our focus was on civilian need, access limits, and quick operational coordination.

This phase helped us improve prioritisation and ethical deployment logic.

2024
Balochistan and broader expansion

We expanded planning for Balochistan and other difficult-access areas with strong emphasis on terrain and safety.

We also improved coordination for food, medical items, and essential packages.

By this stage our operations were broader, more structured, and more adaptable.

2025
Expansion and technology development

We continued expanding our humanitarian drone network and improving the systems used for aid delivery.

The focus moved toward improving operational efficiency, drone performance, and coordination across our existing regions.

We also strengthened our long-term plans for sustainable and reliable humanitarian drone deployment.

2026
Current phase

In 2026, DRONES4AID continues developing its humanitarian drone systems and expanding its global operational vision.

Our current focus is on improving delivery capability, volunteer coordination, drone performance, and access to communities affected by crises.

We are also working toward our longer-term 2028 and 2030 goals while continuing to build a more sustainable aid network.
